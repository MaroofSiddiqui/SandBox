**1. users**
`id, name, email, password_hash, role_id (FK), organization_id (FK, nullable for admin), status, created_at, updated_at`

**2. roles**
`id, name (SUPER_ADMIN / HR / CANDIDATE)`

**3. organizations**
`id, name, domain, status (active/inactive), created_by (FK users), created_at`

**4. candidates**
`id, organization_id (FK), name, email, phone, status, created_at`

**5. questions** (MCQ)
`id, organization_id (FK), question_text, difficulty, created_by (FK users), created_at`

**6. mcq_options**
`id, question_id (FK), option_text, is_correct, order_index`

**7. coding_questions**
`id, organization_id (FK), title, description, language, time_limit_ms, memory_limit_kb, marks, difficulty, created_by, created_at`

**8. test_cases**
`id, coding_question_id (FK), input, expected_output, is_hidden (sample/hidden), order_index`

**9. assessments**
`id, organization_id (FK), title, description, duration_minutes, passing_marks, status (draft/published), start_date, end_date, created_by, created_at`

**10. assessment_questions**
`id, assessment_id (FK), question_type (mcq/coding), question_id (FK, polymorphic), marks, order_index`

**11. assignments**
`id, assessment_id (FK), candidate_id (FK), assigned_by (FK users), status (pending/in_progress/completed), assigned_at`

**12. submissions** (MCQ answers)
`id, assignment_id (FK), question_id (FK), selected_option_id (FK), is_correct, answered_at`

**13. coding_submissions**
`id, assignment_id (FK), coding_question_id (FK), code, language, run_status, test_cases_passed, total_test_cases, score, submitted_at`

**14. results**
`id, assignment_id (FK, unique), mcq_score, coding_score, total_score, status (pass/fail), evaluated_at`

**15. violations**
`id, assignment_id (FK), violation_type (tab_switch/blur/fullscreen_exit), timestamp`
